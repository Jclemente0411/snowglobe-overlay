# SnowGlobe: launch positioning and Play listing draft — 0.17

Independent app designed primarily for Pixel owners. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Samsung.

## Short description (under 80 characters)
"Christmas lights, falling snow and holiday magic for your Pixel."

## Full description draft
Bring a little Christmas to your Pixel with SnowGlobe — a holiday effects studio with falling snow, glowing vintage lights, festive decorations and easy one-tap scenes.

Choose Christmas Eve, Snowy Village, Vintage Christmas, Arctic Night or North Pole Party. Fine-tune speed, snowfall, color, bulb style and twinkle, then save your favorite combinations.

- Light up your home experience with festive snow and string lights.
- Add a SnowGlobe live wallpaper to your home and, where supported, lock screen.
- Quickly pause effects with an optional Quick Settings tile.
- Choose Classic Christmas, Evergreen Lodge, Candlelight or Pixel colors for the app's own look.
- Use Auto rendering to respond to screen refresh, Battery Saver and heat.

Please note: Android manages lock-screen and notification-shade visuals. SnowGlobe doesn't draw above security-sensitive panels. Some shopping, banking and payment apps block touch input while overlays are present; pause effects to use them normally.

All features are unlocked during this beta. No purchases or subscriptions are active.
